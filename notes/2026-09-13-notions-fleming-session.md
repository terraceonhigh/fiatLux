### Requeening reference (late dry season, no chemicals)
Late summer = worst time to find mites (ratio tips as brood declines; crashes Oct), standard time to requeen (young queen winters better). Dearth → defensive bees, robbing; work at dawn, reduce entrances, feed same-apiary diluted honey.
1. Count, don't look: alcohol wash (½ cup ≈300 bees; ~3/100 line, move at 2 in Aug). Drone-brood fork weak this late.
2. Sort: under line / over line / crashing (deformed wings, spotty brood, crawlers).
3. Crashing hives: kill or combine **first** — robbed-out collapsers seed neighbours ("mite bomb"). Only honest reason to destroy a colony.
4. Requeen rest with **extended caging** (2–3 wks): brood break; ~3 wks after old queen removed all mites phoretic. Optional: oxalic dribble (chemistry can make from sugar) or thymol (botany, from thyme oil) — [OPEN] neither in text; new canon if used.
5. Standard intro: pinch old queen; cage with candy plug between brood frames, candy end up; don't open ~1 wk; eggs = accepted. Push-in cage over emerging brood for valuable queens. >4–5 days queenless → emergency cells must be cut first.
6. Hygienic test: pin-kill 50–100 capped cells, count removed at 24h.
7. Queens travel ~2 wks caged with attendants (shirt pocket). **Clock**: she must spend them within days or lose them.
8. Result arrives after truck leaves → she leaves written orders (cf. last doctor's binders, 04-off-label).
Keeper's real argument: local adaptation; single imported line narrows gene pool; daughters mate local drones → dilution unless she returns.
