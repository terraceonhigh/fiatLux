## Why the back half sits where it does

*Renumbered 2026-08-11 when **the minutes chapter was cut** — Terrace: "leave the frat thread as finished at the ending of Collie and the Historian." Renumbered again twice on 2026-08-12. Reasoning and the surviving devices are in [`spitball.md`](spitball.md).*

- **The rotation solved the Diana problem better than the cut did, and by addition rather than subtraction.** Inserting it pushed Diana's chapter *further* from ch2, which is the thing the minutes cut was meant to fix. It does not matter, because **Diana is now on the page in *Formulary***, seen clinically by a stranger — so she is no longer five chapters from her only appearance.
- **The recipe and the rotation preview the outside before the machine speaks.** *Isolation*, *Boil Water* and *Druzhina* are all the institution talking — a water emergency, a ward, and a policy decided over mead. **The two chapters before them are a household feeding itself and a town with no recourse**, so the machine chapters get read against that instead of establishing the norm.
- ***Ruts* breaks the run and returns to what *Formulary* opened.** Diana is the person the institution is going to crush; the tonal contrast is the point, and the rhyme with *Formulary* is why the run can be three chapters long without going inert.
- ***Skywave* before *Colt* is deliberate.** Maia's chapter shows a marriage functioning as a partnership of labour — he builds the clock, she determines its rate, neither half works alone. Putting that immediately before Collie engineers a marriage makes the engineering visible without a word of comment.
- ***Colt* is last** because it needs Lewis in college, which is a time skip, and because it cannot land until Diana has had a chapter.
- ***Spatchcock* is contemporary with *Colt*** — Maddy is 18, so Lewis is college-aged in the same season. Sitting them adjacent gives two kinship transactions in one year, one accidental and one engineered, with no comment required.

### The interleave question (2026-08-13) — parked, not closed

Tried on 2026-08-13 and reverted the same evening: *Ground* at 5 and *Vernalization* at 7, breaking the rotation at both joints. The numbering is back to pre-interleave. **What the exercise established, so it does not have to be re-derived:**

- **The fork is about *Off-Label*'s form, not about *Ground*'s slot.** If *Off-Label* is written as **binder entries**, an interleave before it earns itself — the reader leaves Rosa warm and returns to a cold record that accrued in their absence, and the absence is what the format is summarising. If *Off-Label* is written as **scene**, the interleave is a pressure release at the one joint that needs accumulation: she arrives doing the correct thing three times, then starts inventing pretexts, and that slide should not have a comfortable chapter inside it. ***Off-Label* is being drafted first for this reason.**
- **At most one interleave, and the second joint is the one worth breaking.** *Off-Label* → *Notifiable* carries the real elapsed time, and a break there reads as dread rather than relief: compromised, cut away, return and the road is gone.
- **Two interleaves fail on rhythm, not on logic.** Both candidates are the same household, so the pattern becomes town / flat / town / flat / town — alternation, which teaches the reader that a safe chapter follows every bad one.
- **Rosa is a guest POV.** Three chapters, one autumn, then she leaves without Diana. Diluting the run makes her read as recurring cast and softens the expiry date.
- **Nothing may be inserted at slot ≤ 4.** Chapters 1–4 are posted, so an insertion there renumbers a live serial for readers. *Ground* before *Formulary* is ruled out on that ground alone.
- ***Druzhina* cannot take the second joint.** It follows *Isolation* — the relocation is the first **sanctioned** activation and *Isolation*'s is their second ever, so the mead cannot precede the hose.
- **Standing regardless of slot: the pillow talk is rumour, not groundbreaking.** Every candidate placement of *Ground* precedes *Druzhina*, so beat 3 would otherwise have the university breaking ground before the conversation that authorises it. Demoting it to rumour makes *Ground* **seed** *Druzhina*, costs the scene nothing, and is arguably better — a couple speculating in bed is more marital than a couple relaying a decision.
