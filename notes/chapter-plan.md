## Why 6–8 sit where they do

*Renumbered 2026-08-11. **The minutes chapter is cut** — Terrace: "leave the frat thread as finished at the ending of Collie and the Historian." Reasoning and the surviving devices are in [`spitball.md`](spitball.md).*

- **The cut solved the swap this section used to worry about.** Diana was five chapters away from her only appearance; she is now four, and the institutional run is three chapters instead of four. The lever got pulled by subtraction.
- **6 breaks the institutional run.** Three, four and five are all the machine talking. Diana is the person it is going to crush, and the tonal contrast is the point.
- **7 before 8 is deliberate.** Maia's chapter shows a marriage functioning as a partnership of labour — he builds the clock, she determines its rate, neither half works alone. Putting that immediately before Collie engineers a marriage makes the engineering visible without a word of comment.
- **8 is last** because it needs Lewis in college, which is a time skip, and because it cannot land until Diana has had a chapter.
- **The obstetric chapter is contemporary with 8** — Maddy is 18, so Lewis is college-aged in the same season. Sitting them adjacent gives two kinship transactions in one year, one accidental and one engineered, with no comment required.

## Hard ordering constraints

1. **Diana's chapter precedes the marriage plot.** She currently exists in one chapter; until she has more page time the marriage plot reads as disposal rather than tragedy.
2. ~~**The minutes follow chapter five.**~~ Moot — the minutes chapter is cut. If the device returns as ch5's closing section it satisfies this by construction.
3. **The marriage plot needs Lewis in college**, so it cannot precede whatever establishes that.
4. **The frats' first mobilisation must exist before ch3**, which is explicitly their second. Strongest candidate is the observatory relocation in ch8's backstory — which would make the first mobilisation constructive and the second an emergency, and would answer the open question rather than adding one. **Note this pulls ch8's *events* earlier than its telling.**
