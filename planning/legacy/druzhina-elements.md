## Title

*Druzhina* — the prince's war-band in Kievan Rus, young armed men bound to a lord by gift and oath, the Slavic counterpart to Tacitus's *comitatus*. Root is *drug*, **friend**. So the word for a fraternity and the word for an armed retinue are the same word, built on "friend," which lands on the *veteres amici* thread. It names what is being absorbed rather than the instrument used on it. Settled 2026-08-12, `spitball.md:598`.

Adjacent register set, if the Cossack framing is wanted (`spitball.md:580`): **`reestr`**, the capped list of recognised armed men, everyone off it a bandit — the document Bréal already forged his way onto. Plus `streltsy`, `sluzhilye lyudi`, `krugovaya poruka`.

Register for the chapter: **Latin against Slavic** — Collie the classicist against a Sovietologist. `chapter-plan.md:21`.

## The argument

Collie plays for **the houses to be inducted wholesale.** Fleming wants the armed young men **atomised** — enrolled as individuals.

**Fleming is right.** A *druzhina* is loyal to a person, not a state. State formation is the business of breaking the unit and attaching individuals directly to the sovereign — devşirme, Versailles, the abolition of the han. Federate a militia and you acquire a faction with its own officers.

Collie wants wholesale **because her power runs through houses.** She has Bréal, an arrangement, and fifteen years of jam. Atomisation dissolves the client and converts her patronage into nothing.

**Bréal is the stake and neither of them is thinking about him.** Wholesale induction makes him an officer of the university with men. Atomisation leaves him a foreman with a machine shop and nobody.

**She loses this argument.** It reopens fifteen years later after the checkpoint incident, with real stakes, and she may have handed Fleming the exact justification he needed to win permanently. `spitball.md` § *The political aftermath*.

## What Collie comes in for

**A place for Lewis in Electrical Engineering**, pursuant to Maia's own old failed search. Ch2 ¶3: *"Maia wanted him in the electrical engineering department, but had to search for someone in the faculty to meet with him and see his projects."* **She searched and did not find one.**

**Which is the two women in a single fact.** One went looking for a favour and could not find anyone to grant it. The other got it over mead in one conversation, by having something to trade.

**The place takes blat. The degree does not.** *(Terrace, 2026-08-25.)* Lewis still has to sit it, pass it, and bust his ass for years. Blat gets you the door, never the room. **Load-bearing for the whole book:** if a degree could be bought outright the university would be a sham and not worth capturing. The corruption is in who reaches the door; the thing behind the door is real.

What it costs Lewis: a tradesman among students, better than his cohort at the physical craft and badly behind on the theory. **The shop teaches what works, the department teaches why.** Relearning from underneath, at nineteen, in public. Plus a household cost — he brings copper home every Friday, and years of study are years of not contributing.

**And he will never be told the price, or that there was one.**

## What Fleming actually wants

**The observatory relocation.** He needs it done, and needs the person who can judge which instruments and logbooks travel.

**Maia's posting was never the ask — it was the sweetener.** Collie offers Maia's expertise the way she has offered people before: six men from Epsilon for Alex's wall, Maddy carrying a jam sample into Bréal's household mid-negotiation. Maia's posting was going to happen regardless — she is the one who rotates, the Lacks parents live up there permanently, and she has a husband and son at the bottom who ensure she comes back.

## The price — Terrace's correction, 2026-08-30

**As first drafted the trade was too cheap for what Collie received.** A permanent place in one of the few live technical faculties cannot be paid for with a few months of somebody else's labour thrown in as a makeweight. **The price has to be Collie's own, and it has to be things that do not cost her that afternoon.** Three items:

1. **Surety on herself, in Fleming's own vocabulary.** She asks for Epsilon to be recognised as a body. He refuses, and instead makes *her* personally answerable for the brothers' conduct — ***krugovaya poruka* applied to one woman**: all the liability of a house with none of the standing.
2. **An unbounded medical claim.** He is old. No end date, because nobody knows how badly or how long. **Not hers to give** — it is Maddy's future, spent in a room Maddy is not in. (Downstream: this is what makes Maddy institutionally unavailable for the fishing-town marriage, cleanly and without a scene.)
3. **She delivers the atomisation herself, in person, to Bréal.** Not conceded in an office — **enacted.** Fifteen years of jam and cake and mead spent persuading his own men to enroll individually and dissolve the thing she built with them. He would know exactly who did it to him.

**Do not price item 3 on the page.** The chapter is two academics and a bottle; the bill arrives later. **The instrument already exists:** ch2 ends with cake baked with huckleberry jam, sent to Bréal via Lewis (`01-continuity-test.md:159`). **The cake stops.**

## Fleming's frame: horizontal integration

The Swartz children sit in **Food** (Nadia — greenhouse and apiary under Nona), **Medicine** (Maddy — hospital), **Engineering** (Lewis — the trade and the EE slot). A house with a hand in each of the three faculties that keep people alive.

**The part worth protecting: nobody arranged that.** Lewis went to the shop instead of the department. Nadia grows things because Nona does. Maddy became a medic steaming her dying mother's syringes at ten. **The house acquired horizontal integration by accident, out of three children following their own griefs, and Collie is merely the one who noticed.** She is an opportunist, not an architect, and that distinction is most of what keeps her sympathetic.

**But in a horizontal-integration frame the Engineering hand is Alex, not Lewis.** Lewis is a trainee with a place he has not sat. Alex is a degree-holding, practising electrical engineer. If Fleming is counting faculties, **he is counting the father.**

**Which makes the news harder rather than easier.** She got his son a place in a department. **There is no equivalent gift available for Alex and there never will be.** He will be grateful, and she will know exactly what she could not do.

## Reassignment, poisons to hemp — CORRECTED 2026-09-03/04, was "the demotion"

**Superseded.** This section originally framed Fleming's move as a punitive demotion. **Terrace, 2026-09-03: it is a promotion out of the way, not a demotion — and the draft of `08-druzhina.md` already confirms this framing on the page.** Fleming offers it as field experience toward Nadia's degree, plus a second courtesy (Nona's apiary, via an appropriation bill) to soften it. Collie senses the manoeuvre instantly (*"she is already quite comfortable in the greenhouses"*) but cannot object, because nobody can object to a promotion. Two reasons for the change: (1) Ada being Fleming's granddaughter means a punitive demotion would set a feud running through the couple the book cares most about; a promotion keeps the two houses workable. (2) It removes any grievance that would make the Swartzes want Fleming dead, which protects his eventual off-page death as a pure supply-crisis beat rather than a resolved vendetta.

**Fleming still offers Nadia a menu of harmless-seeming billets as a courtesy** (so the promotion can't be resented), and **Nadia picks hemp herself**, for reasons she works out alone and explains to nobody — his ignorance of what hemp brings stays intact, and her agency is real. The courtesy that keeps the peace is the thing that arms her.

**Original reasoning, kept for the trail — still true that this is a real loss, just recast as the price of a gift rather than a punishment:** A house holding a hand on the analgesic supply, on food, and on a hospital apprentice can decide who suffers — and Collie has already twice traded medical access for favour. Any competent institutionalist breaks that concentration up. **A real cost to the household, not a kindness — just not one delivered as an injury.**

He hands her **hemp** as an administrative reassignment, the safe boring lane to put a demoted grower in. **He does not know what hemp brings.** Nadia works it out herself and goes and gets the billet on purpose — cordage, netting, sailcloth (*canvas* is named for *cannabis*), oakum for caulking, seed oil for lamps and varnish. One crop underwriting a whole fleet, paired with what Lewis and Ada could build and the shipyard already standing in the fishing town from *Wild Oats*. **The demotion and the naval pivot are two different people's ideas landing in the same crop by a coincidence Nadia is quick enough to use.**

**Structural joke underneath, unspoken: a crew at sea is the one body Fleming's doctrine cannot reach.** He dissolves a fraternity on shore and licenses, by accident, the one kind of body his instrument has no purchase on at all.

## The beat to chase — going home

**Lewis is not Collie's to give.** Maia and Alex are alive and in that flat. She counts him — *thirdborn* means she counts him first — and she has **no standing whatsoever** to broker him.

**The scene is not the office. It is telling Maia what she was asked** — a woman about to be sent up a mountain, hearing over the washing-up that somebody wants her son, from a friend who has already decided it is an opportunity, because it is one.

**One announcement lands twice.** Maia gets the thing she wanted, from someone else, on terms she did not set, and now owes. Alex is told his son is leaving the trade, and nobody asked him.

**And he will not object**, which is the whole horror of it. `09-ground.md:131` has Collie needing to tell him to stop calling her *Doctor Swartz*; he answers *"...can never be too polite"* and calls her **ma'am** later in the same conversation. He will thank her and go back to his backlog. Not an argument — **a man being told what is happening to his son by a woman he defers to, while his wife is grateful.**

Note the deference is **his own**, not her withholding. *(Terrace's correction, 2026-08-25.)* *Ground*'s office scene runs the other way: she dismantles the formality herself, seats him, gives him the intact chair, asks his professional opinion by name twice and takes both answers without argument. `09-ground.md:195`: **"Collie let that slide."**

**Staging question, undecided: is Lewis in the room?** `10-wild-oats.md` has the whole household round one table with cider. If he is there he learns his own future from his not-mother in front of his parents. If she tells the adults first, the household knows before he does — the same household that has been keeping Diana from Maia for years.

## Keep in front of every scene

**House Swartz is very minor.** *(Terrace's own check.)* "House" is not their own word — Bréal says *princesses*, Vavasseur says *"valuable university assets and bachelorettes."* One flat, a walled corner of a common room, a greenhouse patch and some hives.
