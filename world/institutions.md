This file owns government, law, custom, offices, affiliation, enforcement,

## Established in the manuscript

- `TEXT` University staff on official duty carry a chit and a metal seal
  while travelling. (`manuscript/10-wild-oats.md:131`)
- `TEXT` House Swartz issues its own copper chit, engraved with the family
  name, and uses it to get a dinner discount at an inn.
  (`manuscript/12-notions.md:1`)

## Terrace decisions

- `TERRACE` A chit is a credential of allegiance, see
  a diplomatic pass or a badge: proof that the holder answers to a patron
  a given town would rather not cross, or would rather impress. Different
  towns recognize different chits because each town has different patrons.
  In principle, an actor loyal to a given house can trust that house's chit
  more than the university's. This generalizes from the one confirmed
  instance, House Swartz's chit (`manuscript/12-notions.md:1`).
