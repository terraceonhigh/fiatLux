## Quantities and assumptions

### UBC Vancouver baseline

`SOURCE` UBC Vancouver is the population model for Arbutus. Current UBC data
gives these approximate populations:

| Group | Population |
|---|---:|
| Students | 60,300 |
| Faculty and staff | 18,500 |
| Normal daytime population | 80,000 |
| Residents in student housing | 14,000 |
| Residents in neighborhood housing | 15,000 |
| Total campus residents | 29,000 |

The resident population is the main collapse baseline. Student and employee
counts overlap with the resident and daytime populations.

### Collapse estimate

`MODEL` The central estimate uses the following population changes:

| Population source | Before collapse | Population after closure |
|---|---:|---:|
| Neighborhood residents | 15,000 | 11,000 |
| Students in residence | 14,000 | 8,000 |
| Daytime non-residents | 51,000 | 4,000 |
| Initial enclosed population | — | 23,000 |
| Later deaths and departures | — | −6,000 |
| Admitted specialists, dependents, and refugees | — | +3,000 |
| Present campus core | — | 20,000 |

`MODEL` The plausible range for the present campus core is 15,000–30,000.
The working value is 20,000.

`MODEL` University country has 25,000–35,000 rural inhabitants. The working
value is 30,000. These people live in towns, hamlets, and farmsteads.

`MODEL` The campus core and rural territory contain approximately 50,000
people in total. This value excludes unaffiliated city residents and remote
trade partners.
