## About the list

The WHO Model List of Essential Medicines names the medicines WHO's expert committee judges most essential for a basic health system: safe, effective, and cost-effective for priority conditions. It is organized into 30 numbered sections by therapeutic area (e.g. "6. Anti-infective medicines"), many of which are further split into numbered subsections. Within each (sub)section, medicines fall on the **core list** (minimum needs for a basic health system) or, where noted below, the **complementary list** (priority diseases needing specialist care, diagnostics, or training). Antibacterials in section 6.2 also carry an **AWaRe classification** (Access / Watch / Reserve) used for antibiotic stewardship. A `[c]` marker in a dosage form (as printed in the source) flags a formulation restricted to children; a note of "square-box" means the source lists that entry with a square-box symbol, indicating named therapeutic alternatives are also considered essential medicines. Two explanatory tables in the source (Table 1.1: age/weight restrictions; Table 1.2: dosage-form definitions) are not reproduced here.

Section 18.3 (Estrogens) is a heading in the source with no medicines currently listed under it. Section 6.8 (Medicines for COVID-19) likewise contains no individually named medicines in the Model List itself -- it points readers to WHO's living guidelines for COVID-19 therapeutics instead.
