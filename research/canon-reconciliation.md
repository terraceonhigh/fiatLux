Door secure. No loss.* The heroism is a door not opened. Nothing I wrote is better than that, and my germplasm material now exists to explain why the arithmetic is real.

### The one place the two sources disagree

The export summary says *"the nineteen are named and the twenty-three are not — inversion of the wall."* Bible §XI says the wall carries the twenty-three and a petition to add the nineteen has been received nine times. Those are opposite.

**Most likely resolution: the wall names the twenty-three, and the lecture names the nineteen.** "Inversion of the wall" is the lecture inverting it — the historian reads out, in the open air, the names the stone omits. That reconciles both sources, it's the strongest possible use of the frame narrative in §XI, and it makes the petition the political attempt to make the stone do what the lecture does annually and unofficially. If that's not what you meant, this is worth pinning, because a lot hangs off it.

**And it retires my posthumous-conferral idea by absorbing it.** I proposed the state's first sovereign act was a degree conferred on a corpse, then recommended attaching it to the twenty-three. Both wrong: the twenty-three are already honoured, and canon already has the better version of the mechanism — **a petition, received nine times, never granted.** Nine centuries of a polity formally acknowledging a question and declining to answer it. That is the same idea, aged a thousand years, and *received* is doing more work than any ceremony I could have invented.

## 2. What each brief is now for

The bible holds the politics. The briefs hold the materiel. That division is cleaner than I expected and worth stating so we stop duplicating.

### `penicillin-city-state.md` → the depreciation schedule

§IX gives the doctrine: **the technical monopoly is a stock, not a flow — convert depreciating stock into durable structure before the stock is gone.** §XII says the sustainable equilibrium is the 1880s floor, and penicillin is a 1940s capability. §XI puts Plutarch's voyage — penicillin across water — at the peak.

So the brief is **the stock's decay order**, and my ranked chokepoint list is the schedule on which the bereavement of Phase 7 arrives:

1. The strain degenerates (no mutagenesis programme, no re-derivation)
2. The blowers stop (deep-tank fermentation dies without continuous aeration)
3. The solvent still loses its copper
4. Cold fails
5. Pyrogen-free water at volume goes, and with it every injectable
6. The assay standard drifts past usefulness
7. Rubber, borosilicate, bearings — the salvage stock §VIII describes, exhausted

The polity walks back down that list to sulfonamides, then to antisepsis and surgery and nothing else, and **calls it consolidation of the curriculum.** The motion passes with amendments.

Two consequences worth having:

- **Penicillin V is what makes Plutarch's voyage physically possible.** Acid-stable, oral, no cold chain, no vials, no needles — it survives a three-month passage in a sealed tin. Injectable penicillin G does not. The whole saint is downstream of one substitution in the fermentation feedstock, and the polity that could do that substitution stopped being able to.
- **§V has Chemistry keeping everything forever** — arsenal, pharmacy, water authority, dye. That's the concentration my brief describes from the inside: the same people, the same solvents, the same still. Nobody planned it and nobody can unwind it, and when Chemistry loses the fermentation plant it keeps the arsenal.

### `university-to-city-state.md` → mostly superseded, and better

§IV and §V do this job harder than I did, and three of their moves beat mine outright: **Parking Services as customs and excise** (I missed movement control entirely), **Printmaking owns a press** as the general principle of asset-holding-up (I had press-as-mint but not the axis), and **Education as the transmission mechanism of the accreditation empire** — teacher certification as more durable than the militia. The four conversion routes — substrate, gate, clients, myth — are the mechanism I was groping at with my five.

What survives from mine as still useful:

- **Facilities and the prestige inversion** — §IV has Custodian as *custos* and Facilities as a guild with keys, §V has Electrical holding the cogen plant, but the *social* fact is worth keeping: the machinist outranks the postdoc, and the people condescended to for a career now allocate heat. §VI Phase 5 says the first serious coup comes from Facilities or the militia, which is that fact arriving.
- **The Library as chancery, and preservation triage.** Canon has the Library fighting the Registrar for the file (§X) but not the physical decay. Digital dies first, completely, silently, while the lights are still on — most of it was licensed, never owned. Paper is acid. **Microfilm is the counterintuitive winner** and the silver competes with the hospital. §VII's descent is the moment that triage list gets written, and §XII's "sheds the knowledge it swore to hold in trust" is what the list is.
- **My Indigenous-sovereignty section is not in the bible at all.** §VII is entirely about villages, headmen and affiliation. In a Cascadian setting the neighbouring nations are co-sovereigns with functioning governments, land bases and treaty rights whose Crown counterparty has evaporated — and §VII's "empire by invitation" and §IX's "charter rivals rather than fight them" both read very differently against a party that has its own charter. This is the largest gap between the briefs and the bible, and it's a gap in the bible's direction.

### `four-beats.md` → the materiel under three canon saints

Beats 1, 2 and 3–4 map onto Vavilov, Lacks and Plutarch/Lamarr. Canon owns the politics of all three; the brief holds what the bible doesn't:

- **Beat 1:** germplasm regeneration — a seed bank is a farm on a rotation, alliums dead in a year or two, potatoes clonal and replanted annually, and damp maritime air the worst enemy of stored seed. That's why 40 minutes of margin per door-opening is a real number and not a conceit. Also **Beal's buried bottles** (1879, Michigan State, still being dug on schedule by strangers) — the bible cites Beal in §0, and the reason it's the perfect anchor is that it's *a seed experiment designed from the outset to be operated by people not yet born*.
- **Beat 2:** the kit and the clinical reality. §VII has the ratchet, the graded archive, appeal as the addictive technology, and triage as the sovereign act — all better than anything in my brief. What my brief adds is what's actually in the pack and what actually kills people: the drug panel, dentistry as a third of the work, obstructed labour and puerperal sepsis and farm tetanus, the glass thermometer whose loss is a crisis, and **the smallpox vaccine carried arm-to-arm in a living chain of vaccinees, which gives the circuit a schedule it cannot break.** Also the failure modes canon lacks: the rider as epidemiological vector along their own route, and the counterfeit rider, which is §IX's metrology problem in a farmyard.
- **Beats 3–4:** chronometer rate certificates, index error, the dividing engine, the almanac office as where the computer scientists went, ship timber as *shapes* rather than volume, teredo and copper sheathing, and the radio asymmetry. §V roots Greenwich in **calendar-definition as a sovereign function**, which is the institutional basis my "time as the third exported standard" was missing.

One correction to my own earlier note, now that §V is in front of me: **receive-cheap/transmit-expensive stands, and Greenwich is the exception that defines it.** Anyone can build a crystal set. Greenwich holds a transmitter, which is what makes it a standards institution rather than a participant — the one party that can answer. Its silence is then a diplomatic event with nobody at fault, because the twenty-metre band closes on the solar cycle and Greenwich keeps calling into it.

## 3. The observation I think is missing from the bible

**Every saint is from the ascent, and the polity that venerates them is the polity that abolished their functions.**

§VIII says it plainly about one of them: the telegraph decouples information from transport, kills the circuit rider, and replaces him with a resident magistrate who can be instructed same-day — *the practicum-as-intelligence apparatus dies with him.* Run that across the whole calendar of saints:

- **Lacks** rides circuit. The telegraph and the resident magistrate end the circuit.
- **Plutarch** carries penicillin across water. Phase 7 sheds the capability to make it.
- **Lamarr** builds the chronometer. That one *survives* — pre-1880 and comfortably under the floor.
- **Vavilov** holds the greenhouse. 4,118 is standing in the valley in year 1080.

So the martyrology splits cleanly into saints whose gift persisted and saints whose gift the polity itself abolished, and it does not distinguish between them. Lacks and Plutarch are honoured annually by an institution that made their work impossible and, per §VII, called it consolidation of the curriculum. **Lamarr and Vavilov handed something forward; Lacks and Plutarch handed forward only the example.** That's the grief in §XII with names on it, and it gives the year-1080 lecturer a second argument to lose.

## 4. Open questions I can answer with research

### §II.4 — coal within 100 km. **Yes, if the setting is southern Vancouver Island, and by water.**

This is the map decision that sets every number in §VIII, and real geology answers it. Vancouver Island has genuine coalfields: **Nanaimo and the Comox/Cumberland field**, worked hard from the 1850s, the basis of the Dunsmuir fortune, and exported by sea to San Francisco. Nanaimo sits roughly 100 km north of Victoria overland — and, decisively, **it is coastal.**

§VIII prices overland bulk at 50 km and navigable water at 300 km-plus for a tenth the cost. So coal is not marginally within reach; it is *comfortably* within reach on the Strait, and the answer arrives with a shape attached:

**This settles §II.5 as well — corridor, not blob.** The polity is linear along the Strait of Georgia, its bulk economy is coastal shipping rather than carting, Nanaimo and Comox are the industrial dependency, and the interstices §VIII warns about are the mountainous island interior and the mainland inlets. Which also means the coercion radius is measured in sailing days from the naval yard, the store's wavefront moves by schooner, and every rebellion still has a correct month.

Washington-side alternatives if you'd rather: Bellingham, Black Diamond, Roslyn, Centralia. All real, all worked historically.

### §II.1 — governance template. **Commonwealth, nearly free.**

If the Academy is a British Columbia university, the dyarchy in §IV is not a design choice, it's the existing statutory structure: a ceremonial **Chancellor**, an executive **Vice-Chancellor**, a **Senate**, a **Board of Governors**, and — the load-bearing one — **Convocation as a real constitutional body** that elects the Chancellor. §IV's doge and §VI Phase 5's "Convocation hardens into an assembly" are then developments of an existing organ rather than inventions. The American template would require you to build Convocation from nothing.

Verify the current statutory particulars before any of it appears as in-world constitutional detail, but the shape is right and it's free history in exactly the way §III's land-grant move is.

### §III's land-grant premise — confirmed, and it decides a thing in brief 2

"If your Academy began as a School of Agriculture and Mechanic Arts, the farm is inside the walls." My earlier brief flagged that in Washington the land-grant is WSU and not UW, and that UBC has a Faculty of Land and Food Systems and a working farm. Canon takes the agricultural-college option, which means **the extension service with agents in every village within forty miles is canon**, and §VI Phase 4's "extension service becomes the instrument of the province, because it always was" is the single cheapest piece of state capacity in the setting. Everything in my Botany section — seed distribution, plant pathology as famine prevention, the herbarium, soil survey into cadastre — sits inside the walls from Phase 0.

### §XIII.2 — Kropotkin's valley. **A third option, and I'd take it.**

Irrigation makes him an engineer and pulls you toward Wittfogel — worth knowing that the hydraulic-despotism thesis is substantially discredited in modern scholarship, which runs the causation the other way: states build irrigation, irrigation doesn't build states. For fiction that's not disqualifying, and the contested version is arguably richer (the water bureaucracy claims a legitimacy the historians can't support). The Cascadian case for irrigation is real, incidentally: Victoria and the Saanich Peninsula sit in a rain shadow with genuine summer drought, so summer water is a live problem in a way it isn't a hundred kilometres north.

The mutual-aid granary is the anarchist reading and makes him a nuisance to the Bursar — which is structurally good, because the Bursar is Odede's office and a standing quarrel with it inherits the founding.

**But the research points at a third door.** Kropotkin's *Fields, Factories and Workshops* (1899) is largely an argument for **intensive market gardening and glasshouse horticulture** — he wrote admiringly and at length about the French *maraîchers* and Channel Islands growers getting extraordinary yields from small plots under glass. So the historical Kropotkin's actual agricultural enthusiasm was **greenhouses.**

Which makes Kropotkin's valley a glasshouse district, and puts him in direct lineage from Vavilov rather than in a separate policy argument — the man whose name is on the intensive-cultivation valley is named for the theorist of exactly that, and the founding relic of the polity is a greenhouse held at temperature off a flue. It keeps the anarchist valence (small holdings, cooperative, a nuisance to the Bursar because it can't be assessed like a grain field), it takes the irrigation problem as a subordinate technical detail rather than a constitutional one, and it gives §XI's martyrology a second horticultural saint who is not a repetition of the first.

I'd take that door. It's also the one that makes the ballad easier to write.
