# Victoria field photos, 24–25 September 2026

Terrace's photos from a trip to Victoria, BC, filed as setting reference.
They are evidence, not canon: nothing here settles a place in the book.

The "Why" column is a `MODEL` guess at what each photo could serve. Mark the
"Keep" column `y`, `n`, or `?`, and delete what you don't want.

- Source: `/sdcard/DCIM/Camera`, originals untouched. Times are PDT.
- Copies are resized to 1600 px on the long edge, JPEG quality 82, with all
  EXIF removed. The set is about 13 MB.
- `Tile` is the number on the contact sheets in
  `/sdcard/Pictures/codex-onceover-2026-09-25/`. Blank means an untiled
  frame from the ferry home.
- Captions come from the trip timeline in
  `School/Courses/JAPN 200/study/victoria-trip-timeline.md`.
- Left out: the stage show and its audience, the man posing at the
  Legislature balcony, framed portraits and group photos of real people,
  selfies and outfit shots, receipts and signs with addresses or phone
  numbers, food, the arcade, and plush toys.
