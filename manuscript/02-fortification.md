## Standard Loaf, version 2daeb1ae

From: Department of Food Sciences, Dietitian Nona Elbakyan

For immediate distribution to affiliated bakeries.

### Formula

| Ingredients                    | %   |
| ------------------------------ | --- |
| Flour, 75-25 wholemeal-oatmeal | 100 |
| Water                          | 66  |
| Salt                           | 2   |
| Yeast, fresh                   | 2   |
| Honey                          | 2   |
| Malt                           | 0.5 |
| Chalk                          | 0.5 |

### Process

| Stage                                                             | Time     | Temp               | Endpoint                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Sponge: 50% of the flour, all the yeast, water to slack batter | 360-720m | 25°C               | Rising stops.                                           |
| 2. Final Mix: Remaining flour, salt, honey, malt, chalk, water    | 10-15m   | —                  | Dough comes together.                                   |
| 3. Fermentation                                                   | 45-60m   | —                  | Dough doubles.                                          |
| 4. Divide                                                         | 15-30m   | —                  | 1000g apiece for a 2 lb loaf.                           |
| 5. Rest                                                           | 15m      | —                  | Moulds without tearing.                                 |
| 6. Mould and pan                                                  | 10m      | —                  | Greased pans, seam down.                                |
| 7. Proof                                                          | 45-75m   | 25-30°C            | Three-quarters tin's height, finger press springs back. |
| 8. Bake                                                           | 30-45m   | 220-230°C, falling | Internal temperature 99°C.                              |
| 9. Depan and cool                                                 | 60m      | 25°C               | Depan and cool on racks.                                |

## The Swartz Carrot Cake

Serves eight, prepare and bring to the bakery after lunch when the oven has cooled slightly.

### Ingredients

12 oz flour, try and get as much wheat content as you can

1 teaspoon potash

6 ounces salted butter

6 ounces oatmeal

6 tablespoons honey

3 tablespoons vinegar, use the mead vinegar.

6 tablespoons carrot, grated.

1 fistful salmonberry or blackberry raisins

2 eggs, whisked.

Your choice of toppings. We have huckleberry jam, but whipped cream is traditional if we ever found some.

Water to mix

### Steps

Mix flour and potash in a bowl, then rub butter into it with your fingers until roughly incorporated. Add oatmeal and carrots and stir in two eggs and the honey. Add water slowly until you have a stiff batter. Butter the frying pan and put the batter in, use the one with our name on the handle. Time yourself 30 minutes, go to the market or do the washing in the meantime. Serve with huckleberry jam smeared on top.

## Maia Tsien's Dandelion Mushroom Noodles

Fill bellies in a pinch before the harvest comes in

### What

Dandelions, yet to bloom

Salt to taste

Garlic with the greens, mince the bulb

Water

Noodles

Dried mushrooms, if the botanists still have any

Ginger, sliced

### How

Rehydrate mushrooms in the pot the night before.

Pick for buds and their leaves, bloomed ones are more bitter. Serve buds straight.

Boil water with aromatics and mushrooms, cook noodles till good. Rinse the leaves in running water, then blanch in the broth for twenty seconds. Serve in the pot.
