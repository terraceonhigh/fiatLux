## Standard Loaf, version 2daeb1ae

From: Department of Food Sciences, Dietitian Nona Elbakyan

For immediate distribution to affiliated bakeries

### Formula

| Ingredients                    | %   |
| ------------------------------ | --- |
| Flour, 75-25 wholemeal-oatmeal | 100 |
| Water                          | 66  |
| Salt                           | 2   |
| Yeast, fresh                   | 2   |
| Honey                          | 2   |
| Malt                           | 0.5 |
| Chalk                          | 0.5 |

### Process

| Stage                                                             | Time     | Temp                   | Endpoint                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Sponge: 50% of the flour, all the yeast, water to slack batter | 360-720m | 25°C                   | Rising stops.                                           |
| 2. Final Mix: Remaining flour, salt, honey, malt, chalk, water    | 10-15m   | —                      | Dough comes together.                                   |
| 3. Fermentation                                                   | 45-60m   | —                      | Dough doubles.                                          |
| 4. Divide                                                         | 15-30m   | —                      | 1000g apiece for a 2 lb loaf.                           |
| 5. Rest                                                           | 15m      | —                      | Moulds without tearing.                                 |
| 6. Mould and pan                                                  | 10m      | —                      | Greased pans, seam down.                                |
| 7. Proof                                                          | 45-75m   | 25-30°C                | Three-quarters tin's height, finger press springs back. |
| 8. Bake                                                           | 30-45m   | 220-230°C, falling     | Internal temperature 99°C.                              |
| 9. Depan and cool                                                 | 60m      | 25°C                   | Depan and cool on racks.                                |
